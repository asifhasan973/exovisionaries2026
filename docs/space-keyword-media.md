## Playback and loading

Only the current card's media is mounted, images use browser lazy loading, and video players load after Play. No keyword media is added to game-start preloading. Slides advance only through arrows, keyboard arrows, or a swipe. Videos stop on slide changes, keyword navigation, closing, orientation/loading pauses, and page hiding. Read aloud and video playback share a single active channel within the drawer. Unavailable images disappear; unavailable embedded videos offer the original Watch video link.

The three supplied YouTube videos were reachable and marked embeddable when checked. Availability, embedding permissions, and external image hosting can change.
